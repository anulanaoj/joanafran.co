i, while counting the i's, am working with perception and consequences thereof. I give to you what I wish to return to myself one day, and think of ways to make clear we are one, through contrasts that lie, saying we are two.
where two opposites meet is where I'd like to be.

currently working with the portuguese words eu dou-te-me, (i give you to me)

my search for a non-physical medium will be here, soon.
