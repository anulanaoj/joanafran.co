selected work

a maker-do-er. speaks in the first person, searches through the instinct to stay still;

works with the body through mark-making, photography and video using paper, fabric and wood; i see myself from outside through the objects i make; measure my body through its tracing on the material, i keep focus on its tension and form.

motivated by relationships between you, me, the chair on which i sit and the spaces where we stand. struggling to differentiate, looks for ways to dissipate borders with attention to subjective experience; and i ask if my border is a trace i can enter.

occupied with somatic resistances which reflect into ways of being; with singularity and the necessity of building aesth(ethics) through an ontological methodology; with re-materializing the body and the space it leaves empty;

, looks forward to holding hands.
