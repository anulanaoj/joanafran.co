When asking friends to navigate this internet space, I am confronted with the absurdity of my internal logic. Where to click, how to navigate, why does this open in another page? They ask.

The work is not to be organized in boxes, website making forces this and I attemp to override, with this internal logic I speak of, through the action of the visitor towards the images. Images that are to be more than just squares to be seen fast fast fast. People seem to not have the time in their lives to navigate the website (concept of website) with the curiosity it deserves. Such a wonderful medium. This touches my motivation to work in the first place. Time for curiosity, time for sitting and looking, thinking, exploring.

You see, I am learning to code by myself, I am stubborn this way. And I like to do things my own way. If a page opens in another tab it is not to bring difficulty but to keep the main page intact, not gone. And back buttons enerve me. If not a back button then it would have to be a written menu which would force to dictate works with a category. Which I hope you can notice is just unfair with my practice. If it was up to me a text would be the same as a drawing (it is shapes on a page, no?)

People have become used to sites which are so chewed all they need to do is follow direction. This is dangerous, it's up to us makers to challange the ease content has brought. Some challange forces exploration. I am not displaying information, I'm curating work, so it shouldn't be accessible through the header, divider, menu logic. Yes things in the field have become so inaccessible we've lost the curiosity of our participants, mine as well, but!! Challenging to think is not to close the doors, is an attempt to open them.
