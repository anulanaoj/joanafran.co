How to add archive images:
1. Put your files into the folder archive_images
2. In terminal, run: node generate-archive-images.js
3. Refresh archiveworks.html

Supported formats: jpg, jpeg, png, webp, gif, avif

Optional auto-watch mode:
1. Run: node watch-archive-images.js
2. Keep that terminal open while you add/remove files
3. archive-images.json updates automatically
