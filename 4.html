go through me go through you
2025, Re:Search:Gallery, Groningen
installation, 4x aprox. 100x150cm, soft pastel on cotton

is a series of drawings taking the place of curtain, requiring interaction, touch, from who wishes to enter a new space

the drawings are excuses for contact. the body, touching the fabric outlined by a pencil, is feeling its borders. interpreting itself as present
the place of contact is one of contrast: where one is made aware of themselves through difference with their counter-actant
this is an in-between space,
which holds the exchange between what is out and what is in. seen in passages that dictate change in environment
