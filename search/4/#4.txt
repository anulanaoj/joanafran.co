ive had the first document ready since the end of july, for some reason I felt not to send it.

considering private and shared, especially after sharing so much with the space between my legs, I felt not the need to share my private,

and I understood I must rearrange how these letters are organized, as I was rearranged myself.


as you will notice, there is a shift, ive simplified things so it is not a bother for me to build (very nice in the start, but I don't think it matters to make this look pretty) and the letter grows organically. 

more of what I share now, at least this time, are writings from this old notebook I found and took as my own, in villa de bank. a wonderful place. if you are curious about the results of my time there, they are on the website. ive found my writing practice, I am starting to recognize my way of being with the medium the same as with writing. I think writing is drawing is sculpting. painting is something else entirely. 

important happenings:

\ It clicked I am a person,

\ found not so much photography anymore -- it keeps me away from the action itself -- I am now to keep recordings,

\ the desire appeared to work with the shared "public" space -- it is a good form to give to my thoughts on ethics, behaviour and empathy,  and making people aware of themselves. special thank you to marta minujín,

\ I am not to hold back from practicing my practice, 

\ I am able to walk on my future again, and have control over it,

\ finding how much control weighs both on the practice and life,

\ seeing how my work is separate from my I. 

growth is beautiful, learning is beautiful.


what I share now was written between last august and this month october.

during these months im conviced I experienced a small amount of insanity, felt hugged by god, felt the wind with the trees, was convinced I could be sustained only by working for a moment, was alone as I wanted and understood I do need to see people, two dates cancelled on me. I was focused with will being better than want, definitions obstructing meanings, destiny (?), and became aware of my insecurity and fear of being.
