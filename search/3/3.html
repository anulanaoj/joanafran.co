i run away from my form to become a form with content. i run away from depicting,

these forms ( i insist on this word because of the Portuguese forma, it is more often used than form in english so has a wider symbolic association to it. forma is as well the tin we bake our cakes in, we say it in ‘ fazer de certa forma’ i.e. which means doing in a certain way. formar, enformar, informar. to shape, to insert in shape, to inform) have become characters in the atelier. they hold a cartoonish posture i feel mocking me. but i equally run away from form with no content, to be just abstract in my case is either i just compose an image or i really run away from myself. what is wrong with depiction when it is unintentional, as well? i do them not with the intention to. and they open their arms at me, and they scream but i hear the inside of my ear.

it stands alone, by itself, without mine or other material’s help. it is the figure of a dive, i like to believe it dives into its own shape. as i try to do with myself. i control this figure. i cut it out, the knife is the inbetween. one hand holds it, the other presses against my arms. i do it out of utility, but notice it does something to the brain. this after the whole of it is singular. it is so mine i carry it from one room to another over my shoulder, at my hips. i feel my belly as i do mine. and i accept i am smaller than i thought, i was not to expect (accept) my two-dimensional self was to feel proportionally smaller than my body. more confusion. and i do this sixteen times.

i move between planes but leave my action always before me. to see the two as three and then four. the four we live in, one forgotten time. i recycle myself between dimensions. overwhelming it is to know there are more, that there are more colors as well, more sensations. and those that can be reached by hovering around the body make inaccessible those righteous to us.

and these pieces i make as an impasse, the hesitation here is something which belongs to my movement as when i walk and get dispersed, and my form dissipates into the street not to be differentiated from those walking past me.
